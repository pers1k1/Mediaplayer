Synced lyrics and a round of fixes from a code audit. Not yet played through by the author, report anything that looks wrong.

**Lyrics**
- The line being sung takes the place of the title and lights up letter by letter with the voice: sung letters glow, the coming ones are dimmed, the letter being sung lifts slightly and settles. Word timings are used where the lyrics have them
- Lines change with the letter morph, paced by the song: the change takes at most 30% of the line, from 0.14 s in fast verses to 0.6 s in slow songs, and the soft edge of the sweep follows the tempo of the letters
- In the card the title moves next to the artist; during the intro, long breaks and after the last line the title comes back
- The island keeps one width for the whole song, set by its longest line; longer lines scroll after the sweep
- Lyrics come from lrclib.net (the title and artist of the track are sent there), one request per track after it has played for a moment, cached on disk, with backoff when the server fails. A candidate must match the title, the artist and the length within 3 s, and junk entries are skipped by checking line count and timestamps
- Settings: Lyrics switch (on by default) and Lyrics offset from -3 to +3 seconds

**Fixes**
- A title or artist made of thousands of invisible characters, formatting codes or stacked accents no longer costs tens of thousands of draws a frame: both are cleaned and capped at 200 characters
- A track without length no longer stretches the pill across the screen with a long artist name
- One write error no longer leaves a track without its cover, and a late picture of the previous track can no longer replace the right one
- The bridge no longer keeps polling five times faster after a session closes right after a track change
- The bridge starts from game folders with square brackets in the path, such as `MC [1.21.11]`
- Huge cover images are rejected by their header before decoding and shrunk to 512 px; metafiles are not accepted
- The bridge library is named by the hash of its source, so a stale or planted library is never loaded, and a half-built one is removed
- Bar colors no longer come from the previous cover after a fast switch; a NaN from the bridge can no longer stop the visualizer for the rest of the session
- Three bridge failures spread over a long session no longer switch music off until restart
- Near the bottom edge the capsule under the island stays on screen
