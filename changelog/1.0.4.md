Line changes without dimming, and held words get an accent. Not yet played through by the author, report anything that looks wrong.

- The last letters of a finished line no longer drop into shadow when the next line arrives, and the first letter of a new line no longer goes through its lift in shadow and then pops to white: during a line change each line now fades at the slot edges by its own scroll, the same way it does at rest
- The leaving lyric line rises and fades as a whole during the first 45% of the change, and the arriving wave starts at 20%, so two different lines no longer sit on top of each other
- A held word (at least 1 s long, at most 12 letters, as in Beautiful Lyrics) gets an accent: each letter grows by 10%, stands higher, glows in the cover colour and thickens like a bold face from its start to the end of the word. Needs word timing, plain LRC lines have no accent
