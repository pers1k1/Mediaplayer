First release of Glass MediaPlayer Island. Not yet played through by the author, report anything that looks wrong.

- A liquid glass media island for the HUD: the track playing in Windows, its cover, a six-bar visualizer that follows the player's own sound, FPS and ping
- Moves like the Dynamic Island on iPhone: springs timed frame by frame from a screen recording; the glass stays sharp while only the contents blur and fly between the pill and the card
- Title, artist and timer change letter by letter; the cover turns over with perspective on a track change
- Synced lyrics in place of the title, lit letter by letter with the voice and paced by the song; looked up on lrclib.net (the title and artist are sent there), on by default, with an offset setting
- Play/pause, next and previous go to the session the island shows, never as a global media key
- Fast track switching keeps the right song and cover
- Earlier builds were called Mediaplayer (mod id `mediaplayer`): remove the old jar; settings from `config/mediaplayer.json` are picked up on the first start
