Kugou lyrics and a brighter sung syllable. Not yet played through by the author, report anything that looks wrong.

- New third source of word-timed lyrics without Spicetify: Kugou (lyrics.kugou.com, KRC format, no token). It covers Western songs missing from NetEase yrc. Kugou search does not understand Cyrillic, so no request goes there for Cyrillic titles. The track must still match title, artist and length
- The sung syllable no longer gets darker than the sung ones on a dark cover: the cover colour is lightened towards white before it tints the syllable, and its glow is a little stronger, as in Spicy Lyrics
- Credit lines like "Lyrics by:" and "Composed by:" are dropped from word-timed lyrics
