First release on Modrinth. Not yet played through by the author, report anything that looks wrong.

- The mod is now **Glass MediaPlayer Island** with the mod id `glassmediaplayer`: the old id `mediaplayer` belonged to another mod. Settings from `config/mediaplayer.json` are picked up on the first start
- The island glass stays sharp while it moves, only the contents blur and fly between the pill and the card
- The title, artist and timer change letter by letter in a wave
- Smooth, evenly spaced type with a light glow instead of the dark outline
- The cover flip with perspective is back, with its own speed setting
- The FPS and ping capsule can be hidden while music plays
- The swell on a new track no longer shifts the contents, and the pill progress bar no longer jerks when a digit changes
